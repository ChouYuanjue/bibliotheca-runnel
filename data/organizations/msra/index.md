# About

Microsoft Research Asia is Microsoft's Asia research lab. Its official page presents it as "Microsoft Research Lab – Asia" and organizes its work through research areas such as artificial intelligence, computer vision, human language technologies, search and information retrieval, data platforms and analytics, systems and networking, algorithms, mathematics, and academic programs.

I list this entry as an external research-collaboration context connected to AI for Mathematics work with Ziyu Zhou. It should not be read as direct employment or a formal internship unless explicitly stated elsewhere. The value of this connection for my work is mainly intellectual and technical: it gives a concrete research setting for thinking about theorem proving, mathematical reasoning, evaluation protocols, and the interaction between symbolic mathematical structure and modern language models.

For this website, MSRA is therefore recorded as part of the research environment surrounding my AI4Math work. The entry is intentionally restrained: it names the institution, records the collaboration context, and situates the work in relation to machine learning and mathematical reasoning, while avoiding overstatement of institutional status.
