# About

THUNLP / TsinghuaNLP is the Natural Language Processing Lab at Tsinghua University. Its public GitHub organization identifies it as the Natural Language Processing Lab at Tsinghua University, located in the FIT Building at Tsinghua University, Beijing, and links to the lab website. The organization maintains a large open-source footprint, including projects such as OpenNRE for neural relation extraction, OpenKE for knowledge embedding, OpenPrompt for prompt learning, OpenDelta for parameter-efficient tuning, and UltraChat for multi-round chat data and models.

I list this entry as a research affiliation connected to my current work in AI for Science and language-model-based scientific reasoning. In practice, this means that my involvement is centered on research engineering, literature digestion, experiment implementation, and the construction of reusable pipelines for scientific and mathematical reasoning tasks. I keep the wording intentionally conservative: this page records my participation in a research context rather than making claims beyond my actual role.

The connection is especially relevant to my broader interests in NLP systems for structured domains. The lab's public ecosystem sits at the intersection of natural language understanding, information extraction, knowledge representation, and large-model infrastructure, which makes it a suitable environment for work that tries to connect language models with scientific knowledge, mathematical structure, and reliable reasoning workflows.
